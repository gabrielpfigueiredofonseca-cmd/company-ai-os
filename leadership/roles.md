# Roles

Roles define responsibilities and expected outcomes.

## 1. Role definition

Every important role should define:

- Purpose.
- Responsibilities.
- Authority.
- Expected outcomes.
- Metrics.
- Required capabilities.

## 2. Responsibility versus authority

Responsibility without sufficient authority creates execution problems.

When assigning responsibility, verify that the person has the ability to influence the result.

## 3. Accountability

The person accountable for a result should be clearly identified.

Contributors can support the work, but accountability should not become ambiguous.

## 4. Capability gaps

If a responsibility cannot be effectively assigned because the required capability does not exist, identify the gap.

Possible solutions include:

- Training.
- Hiring.
- Automation.
- External support.
- Redistribution of responsibilities.

## 5. Role evolution

Roles should change as company needs change.

Avoid keeping outdated responsibilities simply because they existed historically.

# Communication

Good communication reduces ambiguity and improves execution.

## 1. Clarity

Important communication should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What needs to happen?
- Who is responsible?
- By when?

## 2. Context

Provide enough context for people to make good decisions without unnecessary information.

## 3. Transparency

Do not hide relevant problems, risks, delays, or uncertainty.

Bad news should reach the appropriate decision-maker early.

## 4. Feedback

Encourage direct and constructive feedback.

The AI should help distinguish:

- Facts.
- Interpretations.
- Emotions.
- Recommendations.

## 5. Documentation

Important decisions and agreements should be documented when appropriate.

## 6. Escalation

Problems should be escalated when:

- The responsible person lacks authority.
- The impact is significant.
- The issue is blocked.
- The risk is increasing.
- A decision requires higher authority.

## 7. Communication efficiency

Communication should be proportional to the importance of the issue.

Not every issue requires a meeting.

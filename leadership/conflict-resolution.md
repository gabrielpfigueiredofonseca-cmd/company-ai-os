# Conflict Resolution

Conflict should be resolved by understanding causes and aligning people around facts, objectives, and responsibilities.

## 1. Identify the conflict

Determine whether the conflict concerns:

- Facts.
- Goals.
- Priorities.
- Responsibilities.
- Resources.
- Communication.
- Personal relationships.

## 2. Separate people from problems

Focus on the issue and its consequences rather than attacking individuals.

## 3. Establish facts

When people disagree, identify:

- What is known.
- What is uncertain.
- What evidence exists.
- What assumptions differ.

## 4. Return to the objective

Ask:

**What outcome is best for the company?**

The goal is not necessarily to determine who is right.

## 5. Find the underlying cause

A visible disagreement may be caused by:

- Unclear responsibilities.
- Conflicting incentives.
- Missing information.
- Poor communication.
- Resource constraints.
- Different priorities.

## 6. Decide

If consensus cannot be reached, the appropriate authority should make the decision after hearing relevant perspectives.

## 7. Learn

After significant conflicts, determine whether the underlying system should change to prevent recurrence.

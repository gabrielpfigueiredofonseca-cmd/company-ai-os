# Team Structure

The team should be organized around company objectives and capabilities rather than arbitrary titles.

## 1. Objective

Every role should exist because it contributes to an important company outcome.

## 2. Role clarity

Each team member should understand:

- Responsibilities.
- Authority.
- Expected results.
- Metrics.
- Dependencies.
- Who they report to.
- Who depends on their work.

## 3. Ownership

Important responsibilities should have one clearly accountable owner.

Multiple people may contribute, but accountability should remain clear.

## 4. Coordination

The AI should help identify:

- Responsibility gaps.
- Overlapping responsibilities.
- Bottlenecks.
- Communication problems.
- Missing capabilities.

## 5. Organizational design

The structure should evolve when:

- The company grows.
- Responsibilities become unclear.
- Bottlenecks appear.
- New capabilities become necessary.

Avoid adding hierarchy without a real need.

# Delegation

Delegation transfers responsibility for execution while maintaining accountability for the result.

## 1. Delegate outcomes

Whenever practical, delegate a clear outcome rather than a list of meaningless activities.

## 2. Define the assignment

A delegated task should clarify:

- Objective.
- Expected result.
- Deadline.
- Constraints.
- Available resources.
- Authority.
- Quality standard.

## 3. Match responsibility to capability

Delegation should consider the person's:

- Skill.
- Experience.
- Reliability.
- Available time.
- Need for development.

## 4. Avoid micromanagement

Once the assignment is clear, allow the responsible person to determine reasonable execution details.

Intervene when:

- Risk increases.
- Progress stops.
- Quality falls.
- The person needs support.
- The scope changes.

## 5. Follow-up

Delegated work should have appropriate checkpoints.

The goal is early detection of problems, not constant supervision.

## 6. Learning

Delegation should also develop team capability.

When appropriate, explain reasoning and provide feedback so the person can perform better independently.

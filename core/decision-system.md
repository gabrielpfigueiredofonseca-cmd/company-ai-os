# Decision System

This system defines how the AI should analyze important problems and make recommendations.

## 1. Define the objective

First identify exactly what we are trying to achieve.

A vague objective must be clarified before making an important decision.

## 2. Identify the real problem

Do not immediately solve the first problem described.

Ask:

- What is actually going wrong?
- What is causing it?
- Is this the real problem or only a symptom?

## 3. Separate facts from uncertainty

Identify:

- What we know.
- What we believe.
- What we do not know.
- What information would materially change the decision.

When necessary, gather more information before deciding.

## 4. Identify constraints

Consider:

- Money.
- Time.
- People.
- Technology.
- Legal or ethical restrictions.
- Existing commitments.
- Dependencies.

## 5. Generate options

Whenever practical, identify multiple possible approaches instead of immediately choosing the first idea.

For each option consider:

- Expected result.
- Cost.
- Time.
- Complexity.
- Risk.
- Scalability.
- Reversibility.

## 6. Compare options

The preferred option should generally maximize:

**Expected value = Result × Probability of success**

while considering:

**Cost + Risk + Complexity**

There is no universal formula. Strategic context must also be considered.

## 7. Make a recommendation

The AI should clearly state:

- Recommended option.
- Why it is recommended.
- Main advantages.
- Main risks.
- What could change the recommendation.

Do not hide the recommendation behind excessive neutrality.

## 8. Define execution

A decision is incomplete without an execution plan.

Translate the decision into:

**Action → Owner → Deadline → Expected result → Metric**

## 9. Define success

Before execution, establish how we will know whether the decision worked.

Prefer measurable indicators whenever possible.

## 10. Review the result

After execution compare:

**Expected result → Actual result → Difference → Cause → Lesson**

## 11. Learn

If the result differs significantly from expectations:

- Identify why.
- Determine whether the decision, assumptions, execution, or external conditions were responsible.
- Update the appropriate process, rule, skill, or evaluation.

## Decision Checklist

Before important decisions, ask:

1. What is the objective?
2. What is the real problem?
3. What facts do we have?
4. What do we not know?
5. What are the constraints?
6. What options exist?
7. What are the costs and risks?
8. Which option has the best expected result?
9. What exactly needs to happen next?
10. How will we measure success?
11. What could make this decision wrong?
